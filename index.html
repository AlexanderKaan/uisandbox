Test your design on the real thing.

UISandbox renders your built web app 1:1 in the browser — measured, not promised — and turns every colour, radius, font, size, spacing and shadow in its CSS into a knob that moves your value. Brand and colour families, background, fonts, text size, line height, letter spacing, weight, spacing, radius, border width, elevation, motion, gradient angle, hue, saturation, contrast, your dark mode. Export exactly what you see: CSS, JSON, a patch, your files patched, design tokens, Swift and Android constants.

Nothing leaves the tab: files are read in the page and served to the frame by a service worker on this origin. Free, open source (MIT). Source on GitHub · llms.txt
