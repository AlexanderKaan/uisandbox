UISandbox
Drop your build, turn the knobs, export the code.

Play with your real
app's design, live.

Drop your built web app, in your browser or straight from your agent via MCP. Every colour, font, radius and spacing in its CSS becomes a knob; turn them, watch the real app follow 1:1, and export the change as a patch or tokens.

✓ Free · ✓ Open source (MIT) · ✓ Your files never leave your browser

Drop the built site: its dist/, build/ or out/ folder, a zip, or a public GitHub or GitLab repo. It renders 1:1 in the browser; nothing is sent to a server (files are read in the page and served to the frame by a service worker on this origin).

Knobs: brand and the colour families your CSS contains, background, fonts, text size, line height, letter spacing, weight, spacing, radius, border width, elevation, motion, gradient angle, hue, saturation, contrast, your dark mode. Export: CSS, JSON, a patch, your files patched, design tokens, Swift and Android constants. Free, MIT. Source · llms.txt · MCP server
