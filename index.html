UISandbox
Test your design on the real thing

Restyle your app
without rebuilding it.

Try a new look on your real app — in seconds. Drop the build; every colour, font, radius and spacing in its CSS becomes a knob. Turn them, watch the real app follow 1:1, and export the change as a patch or tokens.

Drop the built site — its dist/, build/ or out/ folder, a zip, or a public GitHub repo. It renders 1:1 in the browser; nothing is uploaded (files are read in the page and served to the frame by a service worker on this origin).

Knobs: brand and the colour families your CSS contains, background, fonts, text size, line height, letter spacing, weight, spacing, radius, border width, elevation, motion, gradient angle, hue, saturation, contrast, your dark mode. Export: CSS, JSON, a patch, your files patched, design tokens, Swift and Android constants. Free, MIT. Source · llms.txt · MCP server
